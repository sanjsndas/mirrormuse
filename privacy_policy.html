Mirror Muse Privacy Policy

Mirror Muse respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy explains how we may collect, use, store and share your information when you contact us, book or receive beauty services, or use our website.

Information we may collect We may collect your name, contact details, appointment and service history, and information you provide when making an enquiry or booking. Where relevant to a treatment, we may ask for information about allergies, skin conditions, sensitivities or other health matters. This may be special category personal data, which we will only use where permitted by law and with an appropriate condition for processing. We may also collect payment-related information; card payments may be handled securely by a payment provider, and we may not receive or store your full card details. If you visit our website, basic technical information such as your IP address and browser data may be collected, depending on the website features and cookie settings.

How we use your information We use personal data to respond to enquiries, manage bookings, provide and tailor treatments, keep appropriate business records, process payments, and contact you about your appointments or changes to our services. We may use your information to meet legal obligations, protect our business, and handle complaints or disputes. We will send marketing messages only where allowed by law, and you can opt out at any time.

Lawful bases Depending on the purpose, we rely on the performance of a contract, our legitimate interests in operating and improving our services, compliance with legal obligations, or your consent. Where we process health or other special category data, we will rely on an applicable legal condition and provide further information where required. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

Sharing your information We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with service providers who help us run our business, such as booking, payment, website hosting or customer communications providers. They may use the information only to provide services to us and must protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If a provider processes data outside the UK, we will take steps required by data protection law to protect it.

Photographs We will only take or use photographs of you for treatment records, promotional purposes or other purposes where we have an appropriate lawful basis. We will ask for your consent before using identifiable photographs for marketing, and you may withdraw that consent at any time.

How long we keep information We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business and treatment records, meeting legal requirements, and resolving disputes. Retention periods vary according to the type of information and the reason it is held. When it is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

Keeping information secure We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard the information we hold.

Your rights Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccurate information, request erasure or restriction, object to certain processing, request data portability, or withdraw consent. To exercise a right, contact us using the details provided on our website or booking materials. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns first.

Cookies and website links Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to operate, understand website use or support optional features. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. Our website may link to third-party sites, which have their own privacy practices; please review their policies.

Changes and contact We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available through our website or on request. For privacy questions or requests, contact Mirror Muse using the contact details published on our website or booking materials.
